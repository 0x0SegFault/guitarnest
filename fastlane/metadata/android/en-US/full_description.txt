GuitarNest helps you learn songs on the guitar: tabs, strumming patterns, metronome and tuner. Free and open source. No account, no ads, no trackers. Your songs stay on your phone.

<b>Tabs</b>
<ul>
<li>Write tabs and strumming patterns, or import GuitarNest and MusicXML files</li>
<li>Tab from audio: a song file you own or a recording becomes a draft tab, with tempo, chords, capo, tuning and strumming. The notes are found on your phone.</li>
<li>Play along: the current bar and strum light up with the metronome. Loop a part, play slower, or speed up step by step.</li>
<li>Print or save as PDF for your practice</li>
</ul>

<b>Metronome and tuner</b>
<ul>
<li>Metronome with accents, subdivisions, tap tempo and speed trainer</li>
<li>Tuner with 9 tunings; the string being tuned lights up</li>
</ul>

<b>Your own AI (optional)</b>
Connect your own model: a cloud account (Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, OpenRouter), your gateway (LiteLLM) or a local model (Ollama, LM Studio). It improves the draft, arranges a tab for your level or adds strumming patterns. Only the tab and your request are sent, never audio.

<b>Privacy</b>
<ul>
<li>Internet only when you search for a song or ask your AI</li>
<li>The microphone works only while you tune or record. Nothing is saved.</li>
<li>No song database, no downloads: tabs come from you</li>
<li>Encrypted backups, where you want them</li>
</ul>

This app is developed with AI assistance. The maintainer reviews and tests all code.
