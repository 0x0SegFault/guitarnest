# Songs, tabs and copyright

Songs and published tabs are protected by copyright. GuitarNest is designed so that it does not copy or share them.

## Rules for the app

1. **Never download audio.** The app and the toolkit never download, rip or record songs from any service (YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, radio...), with or without an account. A song may store a link that opens the song in its own app.
2. **No song or tab database.** The app does not download, store or share tabs from the internet. It has no internet access.
3. **Your own material only.** You analyse audio files you own, or import tabs you own (for example Guitar Pro or MusicXML files you bought or wrote).
4. **Results stay on your phone.** A tab the app makes from your audio is for your own practice. The app has no share-to-community function.
5. **Printing is for personal use.** Printed tabs show the source you entered and the note "For personal practice only".

Spotify's terms forbid putting its content into any machine-learning or AI model (Developer Policy, 15 May 2025), and YouTube's terms forbid downloads outside its own features. GuitarNest follows these terms.

## Rules for this repository

- Do not commit copyrighted songs, recordings or tabs, also not as test data.
- Test pieces must be written for the project, or use data with an open licence (for example GuitarSet, CC BY 4.0), with the licence named.

This page is not legal advice. Laws differ between countries.
