GuitarNest helps you learn songs on the guitar: tabs, strumming patterns, metronome and tuner. Free and open source. No account, no ads, no trackers. Your songs stay on your phone.

Tabs
• Write tabs and strumming patterns, or import GuitarNest and MusicXML files
• Tab from audio: a song file you own or a recording becomes a draft tab, with tempo, chords, capo, tuning and strumming. The notes are found on your phone.
• Play along: the current bar and strum light up with the metronome. Loop a part, play slower, or speed up step by step.
• Print or save as PDF for your practice

Metronome and tuner
• Metronome with accents, subdivisions, tap tempo and speed trainer
• Tuner with 9 tunings; the string being tuned lights up

Your own AI (optional)
Connect your own model: a cloud account (Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, OpenRouter), your gateway (LiteLLM) or a local model (Ollama, LM Studio). It improves the draft, arranges a tab for your level or adds strumming patterns. Only the tab and your request are sent, never audio.

Privacy
• Internet only when you search for a song or ask your AI
• The microphone works only while you tune or record. Nothing is saved.
• No song database, no downloads: tabs come from you
• Encrypted backups, where you want them

Built with free software: Basic Pitch (Spotify, Apache-2.0), Kotlin, SwiftUI, MusicBrainz and more. Full list in the app (About and permissions → Open-source credits) and in CREDITS.md.

This app is developed with AI assistance. The maintainer reviews and tests all code.
