# Code of Conduct

GuitarNest follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming. Players of every level are welcome: be kind and never judge.
- No harassment, discrimination, or sharing of other people's private information.
- Focus on what is best for the people using the app.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer through a
[private report](https://github.com/0x0SegFault/guitarnest/security/advisories/new) or by contacting
[@0x0SegFault](https://github.com/0x0SegFault). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may
remove comments, close issues or block contributors who do not follow this code.
