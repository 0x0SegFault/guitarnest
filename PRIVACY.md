# Privacy policy

GuitarNest collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. It cannot connect to the internet.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) | The tuner hears your guitar. | Only while the tuner is open. You can refuse it; the rest of the app still works. |

No other permissions. The app removes the internet permission at build time, and CI fails if a new permission appears.

## Audio

The tuner processes sound in memory, many times per second. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.

## Data on your phone

The app saves your settings (tuning, reference pitch) on your phone only. Android cloud backup and device transfer are off for this app.
